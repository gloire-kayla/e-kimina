g# Evidence and methodology

Prepared 28 October 2026. This is a secondary-source evidence brief, not a respondent-level survey analysis or an impact evaluation. A [reproducible comparison of published saving-channel estimates](../analysis/README.md) now provides a small input CSV, Python script, calculated changes and a chart.

## Problem and proposed contribution

e-Kimina supports the digital management of existing Rwandan savings groups. It records external contributions and member loans, with treasurer confirmation before payments count. Kinyarwanda, English and French interfaces aim to make these workflows understandable.

The intended contribution is clearer records and accountability. Improved financial inclusion, increased savings and reduced poverty are outcomes to investigate, not demonstrated results.

## Method used

1. Identify a relevant study through the NISR catalogue.
2. Read its published aggregate findings and retain their stated population, year and source pages.
3. Map the findings to a product rationale, explicitly identifying interpretation limits.
4. Keep official survey evidence separate from fictional demo transactions and software test results.

No respondent records were downloaded, cleaned, weighted or modelled. The figures above are published estimates. The accompanying analysis calculates percentage-point changes from selected published 2020/2024 estimates, with transcription checked against Figure 35. No district ranking, causal estimate or prediction has been produced. No survey records are joined to member accounts. This limited aggregate analysis may be insufficient for a strong data-science submission.

## National-priority alignment

NST2's digital-transformation section (3.8, printed page 19) and financial-sector section (3.9, printed page 20) provide relevant policy context for digital services and domestic savings. e-Kimina proposes a contribution to these priorities; this is not government endorsement or evidence of national impact. [Official NST2 abridged strategy](https://www.minecofin.gov.rw/fileadmin/user_upload/Minecofin/Publications/STRATEGIES/NST_2/NST2_2024-2029_Abridged.pdf).

## Validation still needed

- Interview savings-group members and treasurers to establish actual recordkeeping difficulties. The cited statistics do not establish the prevalence of lost records or disputes.
- Test contribution recording, confirmation and loan lookup with participants in their preferred language. Report participant count, task completion and errors, with consent and anonymisation.
- Compare record accuracy and task time against each group's existing process before claiming improvement.
- For stronger analytical work, define a specific question, obtain permitted data, document variables and missing values, and use survey weights and design information for any population estimates. Publish reproducible code and limitations.

These are planned activities, not completed research. The public demo contains fictional records only.
